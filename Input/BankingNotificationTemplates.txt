BANKING NOTIFICATION TEMPLATES

These messages are templates. Replace placeholders with values
from the current account through pointers/references.

Available placeholders:
{AccountNumber}
{ClientName}
{Amount}
{Balance}
{DepositAmount}
{CreditSum}
{InterestRate}
{DurationMonths}
{RecipientAccount}
{TransactionID}
{Date}
{Time}
{Reason}

1. ACCOUNT LOGIN

SMS: Hello {ClientName}. You have successfully logged in to account {AccountNumber}.

SMS: Security alert: An unsuccessful login attempt was detected for account {AccountNumber}.

SMS: Your account {AccountNumber} has been temporarily blocked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.

2. ADDING MONEY / DEPOSITING FUNDS

SMS: {ClientName}, funds were successfully added to account {AccountNumber}. Amount: {Amount}. Current balance: {Balance}.

SMS: Your deposit operation was completed successfully. Account: {AccountNumber}. Deposited amount: {DepositAmount}. Current balance: {Balance}.

SMS: The funds could not be added to account {AccountNumber}. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: Your deposit request is being processed. Account: {AccountNumber}. Amount: {Amount}.

3. WITHDRAWAL / EXTRACTING MONEY

SMS: {ClientName}, your withdrawal was successful. Amount withdrawn: {Amount}. Remaining balance: {Balance}.

SMS: Cash withdrawal completed for account {AccountNumber}. Amount: {Amount}. Current balance: {Balance}.

SMS: The withdrawal operation was declined. Account: {AccountNumber}. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: Withdrawal could not be completed because of insufficient funds. Current balance: {Balance}.

4. TRANSFERS

SMS: Transfer completed successfully. Amount: {Amount}. Recipient account: {RecipientAccount}. Remaining balance: {Balance}.

SMS: You have received a transfer of {Amount}. Your current balance is {Balance}.

SMS: The transfer from account {AccountNumber} to account {RecipientAccount} was unsuccessful. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: Transfer request is being processed. Amount: {Amount}. Transaction ID: {TransactionID}.


5. CREDIT APPLICATION


SMS: Your credit application has been submitted. Requested amount: {CreditSum}. Duration: {DurationMonths} months.

SMS: Credit application for account {AccountNumber} is being reviewed.

SMS: Your credit application has been approved. Approved amount: {CreditSum}. Interest rate: {InterestRate}%.

SMS: Your credit application has been rejected. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: Your credit application requires additional information. Reason: {Reason}.


6. CREDIT PAYMENTS

SMS: Credit payment completed successfully. Amount paid: {Amount}. Remaining balance: {Balance}.

SMS: Your upcoming credit payment is due on {Date}. Payment amount: {Amount}.

SMS: Your credit payment is overdue. Please review your payment details.

SMS: Credit payment could not be processed. Reason: {Reason}.

7. QR PAYMENTS

SMS: QR payment completed successfully. Amount: {Amount}. Remaining balance: {Balance}.

SMS: QR payment was declined. Amount: {Amount}. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: A QR payment request is waiting for confirmation. Amount: {Amount}.

SMS: QR payment received successfully. Amount: {Amount}. Current balance: {Balance}.

8. ACCOUNT OPERATIONS

SMS: Your account information has been updated successfully.

SMS: Your account {AccountNumber} has been activated.

SMS: Your account {AccountNumber} has been terminated.

SMS: Your social banking account has been opened successfully. Username: {ClientName}.

SMS: Your social banking account information has been updated.

9. SECURITY NOTIFICATIONS

SMS: A new operation was detected on account {AccountNumber}. Amount: {Amount}. Date: {Date}. Time: {Time}.

SMS: A suspicious operation was detected. Please verify the activity on your account.

SMS: Your password has been changed successfully.

SMS: Your password could not be changed. Reason: {Reason}.

10. GENERAL OPERATION RESULTS
SMS: Operation completed successfully. Account: {AccountNumber}. Current balance: {Balance}.

SMS: Operation failed. Account: {AccountNumber}. Reason: {Reason}.

SMS: Your request is being processed. Transaction ID: {TransactionID}.

SMS: No operation was performed because the request was cancelled.
